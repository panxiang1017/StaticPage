Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Book of Answers ("we", "our", or "the App") handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information.

The App does not require registration, login, or any personal data to function. All app data (answer history, preferences) is stored locally on your device only.

2. Data Storage

All data generated by your use of the App is stored exclusively on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. We do not have access to this data, and it is never transmitted to any server.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following Apple services:

  • StoreKit — for processing in-app subscription purchases. Payment is handled entirely by Apple. We do not have access to your payment information.
  • WidgetKit — for home screen widgets. Widget data is processed locally.

We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

4. In-App Purchases

The App offers an optional auto-renewable subscription ("Premium - Unlimited Daily Use"). Subscription purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect or store any payment information. For details on how Apple handles your payment data, please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Camera Usage

If you use the receipt scanning feature, the App accesses your device camera solely to capture receipt images for OCR processing. All image processing is performed locally on your device using Apple's Vision framework. No images are transmitted to any server.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. The App is rated 4+ and contains no objectionable content.

7. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade any user data with third parties. There is no user data to share because we do not collect any.

8. Data Retention

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over your data. You can delete all app data at any time through the App's Settings or by deleting the App.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
