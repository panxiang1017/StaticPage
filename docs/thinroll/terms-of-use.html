1. What Thinroll does

Thinroll helps you clean up your photo library on your own device: swipe through photos month by month, select old screenshots and screen recordings, find duplicate and similar shots, review bursts, list large videos and optionally compress videos. The app only marks items; nothing is deleted until you confirm in the iOS system dialog.

2. Your photos are your responsibility

  • Deletion is always your decision, confirmed in the iOS system dialog. Deleted items move to the Photos app's Recently Deleted album for 30 days, from where you can restore them. After 30 days iOS removes them permanently.
  • Thinroll's "best shot" suggestions, similarity grouping and sharpness scores are automated aids and may be wrong. Review each group before confirming a deletion.
  • Video compression creates a new, smaller copy and marks the original; the original is removed only if you confirm. Keep a backup (iCloud Photos or a computer) of anything you cannot afford to lose.

3. Free features

Swiping, screenshot and screen-recording clean-up, the large-videos list and deleting are free without limits. The free tier includes 50 lifetime deletions from Similar & Duplicates, 3 burst clean-ups and 1 video compression.
