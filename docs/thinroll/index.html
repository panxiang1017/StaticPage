Thinroll: Delete Old Photos, for iPhone and iPad

Thinroll picks the best shot out of every group of look-alike photos and lets you swipe through the rest, one month at a time. Everything runs on your device: no account, no internet, nothing uploaded.

How deleting works

Swiping or selecting only marks photos. Deletion happens on the Review screen (trash icon, top right of Home) and always shows the iOS "Delete N photos?" dialog. Deleted photos go to the Photos app's Recently Deleted album for 30 days, where you can restore them.

Frequently asked questions

  • Thinroll only sees some of my photos. You granted "Selected Photos" access. Tap the banner on Home, or go to iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos › Thinroll and choose Full Access.
  • Why did it keep this photo and not that one? Each group shows why a shot was picked (favorite, sharpness, faces, resolution). Tap any other photo in the group to make it the one you keep.
  • Analysis seems slow. Similar & Duplicates runs entirely on your device and can be paused at any time; it remembers what it already analyzed. Photos stored only in iCloud must be downloaded first, so speed also depends on your connection.
  • I bought Pro on another device. Open Settings › Restore Purchases. Subscriptions are managed in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions; refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • What is free? Swiping, screenshot and screen-recording clean-up, large videos and deleting, without limits. Pro lifts three caps: unlimited Similar & Duplicates, unlimited bursts, unlimited video compression.
  • Does Thinroll upload my photos? No. The app has no network access at all.
