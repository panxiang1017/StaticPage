Thinroll Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Nothing leaves your iPhone. Thinroll collects no data, has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no network access.

Thinroll ("the app") is a photo clean-up app for iPhone and iPad made by Xiang Pan. This policy explains, in plain words, what the app does with your information.

1. We collect no data

Thinroll does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data. The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking code, and it never connects to the internet. There is no account to create and no email to enter. The App Store privacy label for Thinroll is "Data Not Collected".

2. What happens on your device

  • Your photos and videos are read through Apple's Photos framework only after you grant permission. They are analyzed on your device (for example to detect duplicates, similar shots, blurriness or faces) using Apple's on-device frameworks (Vision, Core Image, AVFoundation). The results of that analysis, such as feature vectors, hashes and sharpness scores, are cached in the app's private storage so the analysis does not have to run twice. They are never transmitted anywhere.
  • Your decisions (keep / delete / later marks and your progress) are stored in the app's private database on your device so you can continue later.
  • Deleting photos always goes through the iOS system confirmation dialog. Deleted photos move to the Photos app's Recently Deleted album for 30 days, where you can restore them.
  • Storage information (free and total space) is read to show the storage header. Location metadata of a video is only copied onto its compressed copy inside your own library.
  • Settings such as onboarding state and the screenshot age threshold are kept in the app's local preferences.

Deleting the app removes everything above.

3. Purchases

Thinroll Pro is sold through the App Store as a weekly, monthly or yearly subscription or as a one-time lifetime purchase, handled entirely by Apple. Apple processes the payment and sends the app a receipt that stays on your device. Thinroll does not receive your name, payment details or Apple Account, and does not run a server. Apple's handling of your purchase is described in Apple's privacy policy. Family Sharing is available for the lifetime purchase. Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.

4. Children

Thinroll does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

5. Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date. Because the app has no network access, nothing in the app itself can change what it does with your data without an App Store update.
